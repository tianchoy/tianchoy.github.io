I'm Chinese, and I love this country. I try to keep things simple. I write code for a living, but I'd rather be documenting everyday life with my phone — little moments, ordinary scenes, things I don't want to forget.

I've always loved telling stories, and that hasn't changed as I've gotten older. If anything, I appreciate them more now.

I also cross-post my writing over on [Essay](https://www.essay.ink/tianchao). If you're on [Fanfou](https://fanfou.com/tianchoy), that's where the day-to-day stuff lives. And if you want to get in touch, just [email](mailto:tianchoy@live.com) me — I'm always happy to hear from people.

Off the clock: comedies, thrillers and detective shows at home, rock on the speakers, playing pool, and watching the Premier League. Plus whichever chicken dish I can get my hands on — I'm always up for a good chicken meal.

Thanks for stopping by. I hope you find something here you enjoy.
